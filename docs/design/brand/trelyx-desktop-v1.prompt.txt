Initial generation prompt:
Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished final desktop application icon for Trelyx, a local-first mind-mapping desktop app with both 2D and 3D views. Square 1024 x 1024 composition. Single icon only, no presentation sheet, no words, no letters outside the symbol, no captions, no watermark. The icon has a clean warm-white porcelain rounded-square tile, front-on, taking about 86% of the square canvas, with gently continuous macOS-like corners and extremely restrained dimensional edge shading. Outside the white tile is genuine transparent alpha, no ground or scene. The white tile is the logo's white background. Center a bold custom violet symbol that unifies a capital T, a growing tree, and a tiny mind map into ONE coherent memorable silhouette: a thick vertical central stem with a softly rounded foot, branching near its upper half into left and right upward-curving arms ending in broad rounded pill-shaped terminals. A third shorter upright terminal emerges from the middle. Use only three terminal shapes and one substantial trunk. The left and right arms should form a balanced yet slightly lively T-like treetop, sophisticated architectural curves, generous open negative space separating the branches, bold enough to remain legible at 32 pixels. It must read as an original designed brand mark rather than a standard share/network icon or literal detailed tree. Rich violet #7545E8 with subtle lavender highlights on upper-facing edges and slightly darker violet lower edges, nearly flat, tactile and precise, no neon glow or glass transparency. Optical centering, symbol occupies 58% of tile width and 60% of tile height. Calm premium creative productivity aesthetic. No leaves, no brain, no sparkles, no extra dots, no circuits, no complex node diagram, no tiny fine lines, no heavy extrusion, no tilted perspective. Deliver a crisp usable app icon with clean edges and real transparency outside the white rounded-square tile.

Precise-object-edit for a production Trelyx desktop icon.
